# Review

A milestone (vertical slice, release, or on request). Walk the whole shape.

1. Give every rule in every area a verdict, **promote**, **keep** or **retire**, with evidence from the repo: work that depends on the rule, or work that ignores it. Done when every rule has a verdict.
2. Check every open question against the work since; each answered one becomes a proposed rule.
3. Present all verdicts as one table (id · rule · verdict · evidence). Retirements go through [retire.md](retire.md); newly settled rules go to enforcement.
